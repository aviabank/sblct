How €SBLCT Works On-Chain

Token-2022 on Solana. ISP98 structure. 1% transfer fee. Mint/Freeze disabled.

🛡️

ISP98 Structure

International Standby Practices — the global standard for SBLC conditions: default, expiration, amendment clauses.

🔒

Mint Authority

Disabled. No one can mint new €SBLCT. Fixed supply — genuine guarantee instruments.

❄️

Freeze Authority

Disabled. No one can freeze or seize tokens. Every holder has full control over their assets.

💸

1% Transfer Fee

Built into Token-2022. Every transfer allocates 1% to the AviaTrust treasury for upkeep.

⚡

Solana Speed

65,000 TPS. Settlements in under a second. Guarantee recording is instant, cost is near zero.

🌍

Cross-Border Ready

Compatible with SWIFT, SEPA, CIPS, UPI, PIX rails. AviaTrust bridges banking to on-chain.

🔄 Token Lifecycle

Issue Bank issues SBLC → AviaTrust mints €SBLCT with matching terms
Record Conditions (default, expiration, amendment) stored on-chain via Token-2022 metadata
Active €SBLCT circulates as proof of guarantee — verifiable on Solscan by any party
Claim If default occurs — smart contract triggers claim flow to beneficiary
Expire At maturity, if no claim — €SBLCT is burned, obligations fulfilled

⚠️ Legal Notice

€SBLCT is a digital representation of an SBLC. It is NOT a bank-issued guarantee.

The legal obligation remains with the issuing bank. The token serves as on-chain proof and automation layer.
