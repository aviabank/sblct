📋 SBLC Models

4 Types of Standby LC

Standby Letters of Credit come in different structures. €SBLCT supports all four — each serves a different stage of the aviation leasing flow.

01
🏦

Performance SBLC

Guarantee of performance

Bank guarantees that a contractor will fulfill a contractual obligation. If the contractor fails — the bank pays.

  • Used for maintenance contracts
  • Used for engine overhauls
  • ISP98 structure
  • Beneficiary: lessor
02
💳

Payment SBLC

Guarantee of payment

Bank guarantees payment obligations. Most common in aircraft leasing — protects lessor against lessee default.

  • Monthly lease payments
  • Security deposit guarantee
  • Direct payment to lessor
  • Beneficiary: lessor
03
🔧

Advance Payment SBLC

Guarantee of refund

Bank guarantees that if goods/services are not delivered, the advance payment will be returned to the buyer.

  • Deposit for aircraft purchase
  • Pre-delivery payments (PDP)
  • Refund protection
  • Beneficiary: buyer
04
📜

Documentary SBLC

Guarantee via documents

Bank guarantees payment upon presentation of specified documents. Common in parts supply contracts.

  • Bill of lading
  • Certificate of origin
  • Inspection certificate
  • Beneficiary: supplier

🛡️ How €SBLCT Tokenizes an SBLC

1
Applicant (airline or lessee) requests SBLC from issuing bank.
2
Issuing bank issues the SBLC with ISP98 conditions — default, expiration, amendment clauses.
3
AviaTrust records the SBLC terms on-chain as a €SBLCT token.
4
Beneficiary (lessor or supplier) receives the €SBLCT as on-chain proof of guarantee.
5
If default occurs — the smart contract triggers claim flow. If no default — €SBLCT expires at maturity.

⚠️ Important

€SBLCT is a digital representation of a Standby Letter of Credit. It is NOT a bank-issued guarantee.

The token records the terms of the guarantee on-chain, but the legal obligation remains with the issuing bank.
